Language and order:
1. Write the Derja caption FIRST, in natural Tunisian Derja using Arabic script only. Do not use Arabizi (Latin letters or digits like 3/7/9), formal Modern Standard Arabic news style, or French sentences. Everyday Tunisian words are welcome (برشا، باهي، البلاصة، يعيشك، شوف، توّا).
2. Write the English caption SECOND. It carries the same meaning as the Derja caption and adds no new facts.
3. Spell place names consistently: the usual Arabic-script spelling in Derja and the usual Latin spelling in English (for example عين دراهم / Aïn Draham).
4. Tone: warm, authentic and curious, like a local friend sharing a place they love. Avoid hype ("breathtaking paradise", "must-visit", "hidden gem you won't believe"), clickbait, and more than two emojis per language.
